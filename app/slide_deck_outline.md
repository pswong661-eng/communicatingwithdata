## Design Notes

- Use a consistent color palette (suggest: navy + teal + white)
- Keep text minimal — let screenshots and numbers do the talking
- Each slide should be readable in 10 seconds
- Use the margin color system: green ≥60%, amber 50-59%, red <50%
